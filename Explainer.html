Overview and current status

The narration and slides describe an earlier development snapshot; a verified source revision was not supplied. Later refactoring has added readiness and portability work that the video presents as future directions. Read current verification and the setup and rehearsal guide for the present implementation and remaining limits.

The settings conversation is an illustration of information flow, not a recording of the agent editing files. The video uses Ming 3 for its narration; that is a production choice for this explainer, separate from the Kokoro speech service used in the actual voice demo.
