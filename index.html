What is Korina?

Korina is two related systems sharing one local runtime. Korina Converse is the voice-first design chat channel. Korina Agent is the local-first agent built for it. Korina is designed to keep your voice data and inference on your own hardware — and it can use cloud-compatible endpoints when explicitly configured.
