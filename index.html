What is Korina?

Korina is two related systems sharing one local runtime. Korina Converse is the voice-first design chat channel. Korina Agent is the local-first agent built for it. Korina is designed to keep your voice data and inference on your own hardware — but it can fall back to cloud providers when local resources are insufficient.

🗣 Korina Converse

Browser-based voice UI: live VAD, rolling partial transcription, response LLM, Kokoro TTS, barge-in, and voice interrupts.
