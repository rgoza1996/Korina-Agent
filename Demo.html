What ran

Response

Direct Meta Model API, muse-spark-1.3-contributor, minimal reasoning effort and a deliberate 2048-token budget. Agent reports were off.

Local Kokoro af_heart on CPU. Both answer chunks reached native browser ended events; Stop released capture.
